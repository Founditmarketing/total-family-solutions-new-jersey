Meet Total Family Solutions New Jersey

Who We Are

Established in 2003, Total Family Solutions is a therapist-owned and operated provider dedicated to supporting children, adults, couples, and families in achieving meaningful growth and well-being. We believe that every individual has unique strengths, and with proper guidance and support, they can overcome challenges and thrive.

Our services are individually developed to meet the specific needs of each client and are implemented in accordance with a personalized plan of care. Utilizing a short-term, solution-focused approach, we provide adult-, child-, and family-centered care that emphasizes strengths, resilience, and positive outcomes.

As a New Jersey Medicaid Approved Provider, Total Family Solutions also accepts select private insurances, ensuring our high-quality behavioral and mental health services are accessible to a broad range of families. Through our strength-based, evidence-informed approach, we empower individuals and families to build confidence, improve relationships, and achieve lasting success.
